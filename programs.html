What we do, in detail

Four services, all processed to the garment's care label.

Program 01 · What the label allows

Dry cleaning & pressing

The label is not a suggestion; it reflects what the fabric, the interlining and the trims will survive. Where a customer wants a process the label forbids we will do it only at their written request and at their risk, because the result is frequently a shrunk or distorted garment.

  • 2 to 4 days
  • Everyone
  • Per item
  • Care label present

Program 02 · On a weekly rhythm

Laundered shirts

Collars and cuffs need treating before the wash rather than after, which is why some shirts come back with a grey line and some do not. Regular weekly collection works out cheaper per shirt and means we learn how you want them finished.

  • 24 to 48 hours
  • Everyone
  • Per shirt
  • Nothing to prepare

Program 03 · Fitted, then pinned

Alterations & repairs

Alterations are pinned on you wherever possible, because a measurement taken flat rarely produces the result someone pictured. We will also tell you when an alteration is not worth doing — letting out a garment with no seam allowance simply cannot be done well.

  • 3 to 7 days
  • Everyone
  • Per alteration
  • A fitting

Program 04 · Sooner is better

Wedding dress cleaning

Champagne, sugar and perspiration oxidise into brown marks over months, so a dress cleaned a year later rarely comes back as well as one cleaned in a fortnight. We are honest about what is achievable on an older dress rather than taking the fee and hoping.

  • 2 to 4 weeks
  • Brides
  • Quoted per dress
  • Bring it soon
